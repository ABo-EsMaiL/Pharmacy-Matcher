For OpenCode's Inference API, the base URL for the OpenAI-compatible chat completions endpoint is:

**`https://opencode.ai/inference/openai/v1/chat/completions`**[1][2]

### Key Details for Usage:
*   **Endpoint Path:** `POST /inference/openai/v1/chat/completions`[2]
*   **Authentication:** Requires an `Authorization: Bearer <token>` header, where `<token>` is your service account key[1][2]. Note that some free chat models may be accessible without this header[2].
*   **Other Endpoints:** Depending on the specific service or model you are accessing (such as their "Go" or "Zen" model sets), you may encounter other endpoints like `https://opencode.ai/zen/go/v1/chat/completions` or `https://opencode.ai/zen/v1/chat/completions`[3][4][5]. 

If you are configuring a custom provider in your `opencode.json` configuration file, ensure your `baseURL` points to the appropriate versioned endpoint (e.g., ending in `/v1`)[6].
